## Plan

Select one:

- Starter: entry plan for one clinic, limited channels, seats, monthly messages,
  AI usage, and standard support.
- Growth: recommended plan for an operating clinic with full front-desk recovery
  workflows and AI assist.
- Scale: multi-location plan with higher seat, channel, message, AI, and support
  limits.

Plan limits must match the public pricing page on the signature date.

## Trial and Activation

- Trial length: 14 days.
- During trial: workspace, inbox, setup, billing, and demo recovery flows are available.
- After trial: paid actions can lock while billing and setup stay readable.
- Activation: Stripe Checkout/Portal when enabled, or manual invoice with support
  activation after payment confirmation.
