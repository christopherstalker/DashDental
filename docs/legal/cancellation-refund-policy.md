# Cancellation and Refund Policy

This policy is the default launch template for manual invoice or Stripe billing
until a signed order form says otherwise.

## Trial

Clinics can evaluate Dental Recovery during the 14-day trial. No paid activation
is required to inspect the workspace, setup flow, pricing, security, privacy,
terms, and demo recovery flow.

## Cancellation

Customers can request cancellation through the support contact on the order form
or through Stripe Customer Portal when Stripe is enabled. Cancellation stops
future renewal charges. Read-only access can remain available for operational
handoff and billing review.

## Refunds

Refunds are reviewed case by case. Refund decisions should consider duplicate
payments, mistaken manual transfers, service availability, and whether the
customer received paid activation for the period.

## Plan Changes

Upgrades can unlock higher limits after payment or Stripe confirmation.
Downgrades should preserve customer data while applying the lower plan limits at
the agreed effective date.

## Data After Cancellation

Cancellation does not automatically delete leads, conversations, messages,
billing records, subscriptions, or audit logs. Data export or deletion requests
must be handled through the approved support/legal process.
